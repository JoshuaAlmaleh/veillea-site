## 2. Information we collect

**Account information.** When you create an account: your email address, a password (stored only as a salted scrypt hash, never in readable form), your name (optional), your organization name, your preferred language, and your plan choice. We also record when the account was created, whether your email address was confirmed, and the date of your last login.

**Team members.** If an account owner adds a team member, we store that person's email address, name (optional) and role (owner or member).

**Monitoring profiles.** The keywords, excluded words, category codes (UNSPSC/GSIN), regions and sources you choose, and which alerts you have unsubscribed from. This describes what your business is looking for, and is linked to your account.

**Billing information.** Payments are processed by Stripe. We do not receive or store your card number. Stripe collects your payment details and billing address. We store only your Stripe customer and subscription identifiers, your plan, billing interval, subscription status, period end date and any scheduled cancellation date.

**Usage and technical information.** Our server keeps a technical log of requests (date, method, page requested, response code). Requests reach our server through Cloudflare, which processes your IP address to route traffic and protect the Service. [QUESTION FOR JOSHUA: confirm whether any server or Cloudflare logs record visitors' IP addresses, and how long those logs are kept.] We record which alerts were sent to which profile, and account events (for example a trial start, cancellation or trial extension).

**API keys.** If your plan includes API access, we store only a hash of your API key.

**Communications.** If you write to us, we keep your message and our reply.

We do not collect sensitive personal information, and the Service is not intended for individuals under 14.

- To create and secure your account, and to log you in (session cookie, password reset, email confirmation).
- To provide the Service: match public tender notices to your profiles and send you alerts by email at the frequency of your plan.
- To manage your plan, free trial, billing and cancellation.
- To send service messages (email confirmation, password reset, alerts, billing and account notices, and a message when a subscription ends).
- To monitor the Service, prevent abuse and fix problems. The owner receives internal notices about sign-ups and subscription changes.
- To meet our legal obligations (for example accounting records).

## 4. Cookies and similar technologies

**Application (app.veillea.ca):**
- a session cookie, required to keep you logged in. It is HttpOnly and lasts up to 30 days, or until you log out;
- a language cookie that remembers your language choice, kept for up to 1 year.

**Marketing site (veillea.ca):** your language choice is stored in your browser's local storage.

Neither site uses analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.

Both sites load fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser sends your IP address to Google when a page loads. The live tracker on veillea.ca also contacts app.veillea.ca to fetch public counts. [QUESTION FOR JOSHUA: keep Google Fonts, or self-host the fonts so no request goes to Google?]

## 8. Your rights

You may:
- ask to access the personal information we hold about you;
- have it corrected if it is inaccurate, incomplete or out of date;
- withdraw your consent (for example by unsubscribing from alerts or closing your account);
- ask that it be deleted or no longer disseminated, where the law allows;
- ask for a copy of the information you gave us in a structured, commonly used technological format (portability).

To exercise a right, write to bonjour@veillea.ca. We may need to verify your identity. We will reply within 30 days. You may also file a complaint with the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec (cai.gouv.qc.ca) or the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (priv.gc.ca).

## 9. Security

Passwords are hashed (scrypt), connections use HTTPS, session cookies are HttpOnly, and only the owner has administrative access. No method is perfectly secure.

## 10. Confidentiality incidents

If a confidentiality incident occurs involving personal information we hold, we will take reasonable measures to reduce the risk of harm and keep a register of incidents. If there is a risk of serious injury, we will notify the Commission d'accès à l'information, the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada and the people concerned, as required by law.
