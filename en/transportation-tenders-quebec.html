Where transportation tenders are published

In Québec, public bodies and municipalities publish public calls for tenders on SEAO. For 2026-2027, public tendering applies from $139,000 for services contracts of government departments, the health and education networks and municipalities. Below that, a contract can be awarded by invitation or directly and isn't necessarily published. Federal contracts are posted on CanadaBuys.

Common requirements

  • Revenu Québec certificate (attestation): required for contracts of $25,000 or more.
  • RENA: a business listed in the register of ineligible enterprises can't bid.
  • Francization (OQLF): required for businesses with 25 or more employees in Québec.
  • AMP authorization to contract: required for services contracts and subcontracts of $1M or more (tendered or direct award).
  • Transport permits, insurance and sector requirements: per each tender document.
