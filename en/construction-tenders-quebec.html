Where are Québec construction tenders published?

In Québec, government departments and public bodies must publish their public calls for tenders on SEAO (Act respecting contracting by public bodies), and so must municipalities. From January 1, 2026 to December 31, 2027, the trade-agreement threshold (CFTA/QOTCA) for construction and services contracts of government departments is $139,000, and municipalities must use an open (public) procedure at $139,000 and up.

Federal work in Québec is posted on CanadaBuys. Veillea brings both into one dashboard.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I find construction tenders in Québec?

Québec public bodies and municipalities publish public calls for tenders on SEAO; federal contracts are published on CanadaBuys. Veillea monitors both and sends you the notices that match your profile.

What contract value triggers a public tender in Québec?

From January 1, 2026 to December 31, 2027, the trade-agreement threshold for government department construction contracts is $139,000, and municipalities must use an open procedure at $139,000 and up. Other rules may apply depending on the buyer.

Can I read French SEAO construction notices in English?

Yes. Every notice gets an English and French summary on paid plans. The summary helps you triage; the official notice always prevails.

Does Veillea replace registering on SEAO?
