MERX alternative: when Veillea fits better (and when it doesn't)

MERX is a well-established Canadian platform for public and private tenders, with document downloads and electronic bid submission. Veillea does something different: Québec-first monitoring of SEAO, CanadaBuys and other Canadian sources, with ranked notices and bilingual summaries.

MERX at a glance (public site, checked October 12, 2026)

  • Basic: free, giving access to notices from buyers that have paid for your access (e.g., Bank of Canada, Government of Newfoundland and Labrador, Manitoba Government, Fonds de solidarité FTQ).
  • Paid "Canadian Tenders" plans: shown from $50/month for one province (including Quebec), with pricier regional and national plans. Advanced search, daily notification, unlimited downloads and eBid submissions.
  • Private Construction: a separate product, e.g., Quebec $88.00/month or Montreal & surroundings $57.42/month (annual-billing prices).
  • One free matching profile; extra profiles $1.95/month (up to 3) or $2.95/month (up to 9).

Which to choose?

  • MERX if the buyers you target post on MERX, or you want private construction leads.
  • Veillea if your buyers are mostly on SEAO and federal, and you want fast triage in English and French.
  • Both: Veillea doesn't cover MERX notices; if your buyers post on both, many firms use both tools.

Veillea does not replace registering on SEAO, CanadaBuys or a buyer's portal: to get documents and bid, you still use the official platform. Summaries help you triage; the official notice always prevails.

Frequently asked questions

Does Veillea cover MERX notices?

No. Veillea doesn't cover MERX. It covers SEAO, CanadaBuys, SaskTenders and the City of Toronto, plus a few US sources.

How much does MERX cost?

Per its site, Basic is free for notices from buyers that have paid for your access; paid plans start at $50/month for one province. Check MERX's pricing page, as prices change.

Can I bid through Veillea?

No. Veillea finds and ranks notices. You bid on the platform named in the notice (SEAO, CanadaBuys, MERX, etc.).

Why choose Veillea over MERX?

If you mainly target SEAO and federal notices, Veillea brings them together, ranks them against your profile and summarizes them in English and French, from $0.

Can I use MERX and Veillea together?

Yes. They don't cover the same sources, and many firms combine tools.
