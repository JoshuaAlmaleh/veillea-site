Where supply contracts are published

Québec public bodies and municipalities publish public calls for tenders on SEAO; federal contracts are on CanadaBuys. For 2026-2027, the trade-agreement threshold for goods is $34,700 for Québec government departments and agencies, and $139,000 for the health and education networks and municipalities.

Common requirements

  • Revenu Québec certificate (attestation): required for contracts of $25,000 or more.
  • RENA: a business listed in the register of ineligible enterprises can't bid.
  • Francization (OQLF): required for businesses with 25 or more employees in Québec.
  • Invitation to tender: below thresholds, a Québec department or agency should generally invite at least three businesses, including at least one SMB (250 employees or fewer).
  • Technical specs, samples, warranties: per each tender document.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I find supply tenders in Québec?

On SEAO (public bodies and municipalities) and CanadaBuys (federal). Veillea monitors both.

What is the tender threshold for goods in Québec?

For 2026-2027: $34,700 for government departments and agencies; $139,000 for the health and education networks and municipalities.

How do I get invited to invitation-only tenders?

They aren't publicly posted. Québec departments and agencies should generally invite at least three businesses including one SMB. Make yourself known to buyers in your sector.

Can I track several product lines?

Yes, one profile per line: 1 on Solo, 15 on Team, unlimited on Enterprise.
