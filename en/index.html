How our tender alert service works

Describe what you do once. Veillea reads the official sources every day and shows you only what matters.

  1. Set up your profile. Keywords in English and French, categories (UNSPSC/GSIN), regions. Multiple profiles if you have several lines of business.
  2. Veillea collects and matches. Notices from official sources are imported, normalized and matched against your profiles in both languages.
  3. Act on the best ones. Ranked matches, bilingual summaries, deadline badges: decide quickly whether to bid.

Frequently asked questions

Can I get SEAO tenders in English?

Yes. Veillea matches notices against your English and French keywords and gives you a summary of each notice in both languages, so French-only SEAO notices still reach you. The official notice always prevails.

Which government tender sources do you cover?

SEAO (Québec) and CanadaBuys (federal) are live. MERX, Bids&Tenders, municipal sites and the Ontario portal are in development and will be included at no extra cost.

How is Veillea different from SEAO's own alerts?

SEAO's matching alerts cost $6.95/month per Specialist user plus $0.35 per notice sent, and cover SEAO only. Veillea charges a flat monthly price, adds CanadaBuys, ranks notices against your profile and summarizes them in English and French.

Is there a free plan?

Yes. The Free plan covers SEAO with a weekly digest and one monitoring profile. Paid plans come with a 14-day free trial, no credit card required.

Does Veillea replace registering on SEAO?

No. Veillea helps you find relevant notices; to get documents and submit a bid you still use the official platform.

Can I change plans or cancel?

Yes, anytime from your account; charges are prorated. The 14-day trial has no commitment: at the end, pick a plan or move to Free.
