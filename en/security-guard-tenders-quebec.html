Where security contracts are published

In Québec, public bodies and municipalities publish public calls for tenders on SEAO. For 2026-2027, public tendering applies from $139,000 for services contracts of government departments, the health and education networks and municipalities. Below that, a contract can be awarded by invitation or directly and isn't necessarily published. Federal contracts are posted on CanadaBuys.

Common requirements

  • Revenu Québec certificate (attestation): required for contracts of $25,000 or more.
  • RENA: a business listed in the register of ineligible enterprises can't bid.
  • Francization (OQLF): required for businesses with 25 or more employees in Québec.
  • AMP authorization to contract: required for services contracts and subcontracts of $1M or more (tendered or direct award).
  • Bureau de la sécurité privée agency licence and agent licences for staff, per the tender and Québec's Private Security Act.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I find security guard tenders in Québec?

On SEAO (public bodies and municipalities) and CanadaBuys (federal). Veillea monitors both.

Which threshold applies to security contracts?

For 2026-2027, public tendering applies from $139,000 for services contracts of departments, the health and education networks and municipalities. AMP authorization is required from $1M.

Do I need a licence to bid?

A security agency in Québec must hold a Bureau de la sécurité privée licence; exact requirements are stated in each tender.

Can I read French security notices in English?

Yes. Paid plans include an English and French summary of every notice.
