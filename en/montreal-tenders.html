What Veillea covers today

Québec municipalities must publish public calls for tenders on SEAO (an open procedure is mandatory at $139,000 and up until December 31, 2027). Veillea monitors SEAO, so City of Montréal and other municipal public tenders posted there are already covered, with English and French summaries.

What Veillea doesn't cover

Veillea doesn't monitor the City of Montréal's website or other Québec municipal websites directly, and doesn't cover Bids&Tenders. Contracts below the threshold (e.g., by written invitation) aren't necessarily posted on SEAO.

How it works

  1. Set up your profile: keywords in English and French, categories (UNSPSC/GSIN), regions. Multiple profiles if you have several lines of business.
  2. Veillea collects and matches notices from official sources every day, in both languages.
  3. Act on the best ones: ranked matches, bilingual summaries, deadline badges, and filters by source, category, region or date.

Who it's for

Cleaning and janitorial, construction, IT and software, consulting: SMBs that sell, or want to sell, to municipalities.

Live Canadian sources: SEAO (Québec), CanadaBuys (federal), SaskTenders (Saskatchewan) and the City of Toronto. Veillea also covers several US sources, such as SAM.gov.

Compared with SEAO's own alerts

SEAO's alert service ("jumelage") requires the Specialist subscription at $6.95/month per user, plus $0.35 per notice sent, and covers SEAO only. Veillea is a flat price, adds CanadaBuys and other sources, ranks every notice against your profile and summarizes it in English and French.

SEAO fees: fee schedule at seao.gouv.qc.ca, checked October 12, 2026. Veillea does not replace registering on SEAO or a buyer's own portal: to get documents and submit a bid, you still use the official platform. Summaries help you triage; the official notice always prevails.

Frequently asked questions

Does Veillea cover City of Montréal tenders?

Yes, those published on SEAO, where municipalities must post their public calls for tenders. The city's own website isn't monitored directly.

What contract value requires a public tender for a Québec municipality?

Until December 31, 2027, an open procedure is mandatory at $139,000 and up. Between $25,000 and that threshold, rules depend in part on the municipality's contract management by-law.

Do you monitor municipal websites directly?

Not in Québec: Québec municipal tenders are covered through SEAO. The only municipal source monitored directly is the City of Toronto.

Do you cover Bids&Tenders?

No. Veillea doesn't cover Bids&Tenders.

Can I get Montréal tenders in English?

Yes, for notices on SEAO: paid plans include an English and French summary of every notice. The official notice always prevails.

Is there a free plan?

Yes. Free covers SEAO with a weekly digest. Paid plans start at $29/month, with a 14-day trial and no credit card.
