SEAO alerts alternative: SEAO's matching service vs. Veillea

SEAO has its own alert service, jumelage (matching): it runs daily searches on your criteria and emails you new notices. It's a solid service built into the official platform. Here's when Veillea may fit better, and when SEAO's alerts are enough.

SEAO matching alerts at a glance

  • Requires a user subscription: $6.95/month per Specialist user ($3.00 for a Reader).
  • $0.35 per notice sent by email.
  • Covers notices published on SEAO.
  • Bonus: the subscription also lowers some SEAO fees (electronic bid submission $20 instead of $30, no $20 occasional-order fee).

When to keep SEAO's alerts

If you often order documents or bid electronically on SEAO, the Specialist subscription is probably worth it anyway. Many firms use both: Veillea to find and triage, SEAO for documents and bidding.

Veillea does not replace registering on SEAO, CanadaBuys or a buyer's portal: to get documents and bid, you still use the official platform. Summaries help you triage; the official notice always prevails.

Frequently asked questions

Does SEAO have email alerts?

Yes, its matching service (jumelage). It requires a user subscription ($6.95/month per Specialist) and costs $0.35 per notice emailed.

Why look for an alternative to SEAO alerts?

To add federal CanadaBuys tenders, get relevance ranking and English/French summaries, or pay a flat price instead of a per-notice fee.

Does Veillea replace my SEAO subscription?

No. Veillea helps you find notices; to order documents and submit a bid you use SEAO. A SEAO subscription can lower your document and submission fees.

Can I read SEAO notices in English?

Yes. Paid plans include an English and French summary of every notice. The official notice always prevails.

Is Veillea free?

The Free plan covers SEAO with a weekly digest and one profile. Paid plans start at $35/month, with a 14-day trial and no credit card.
