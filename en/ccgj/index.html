Veillea, for businesses in Joliette and Lanaudière

Veillea is a public-tender monitoring tool, built in Montréal for SMBs. It watches SEAO, CanadaBuys and other official sources in Canada and the United States, ranks every notice against your company profile, and sends you the ones that match.

On paid plans, each notice is summarized in English and French, and closing dates are easy to spot. Veillea does not replace registering on SEAO: to get the documents and submit a bid, you still use the official platform.

Yes. As the offer states, the 25% discount on the annual plan applies at every renewal, for as long as you are a CCGJ member.
