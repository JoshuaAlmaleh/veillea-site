TenderScan vs. Veillea: Canada-wide coverage or bilingual Québec-first monitoring?

TenderScan is a Canadian platform that monitors federal, provincial and municipal sources nationwide and adds bid management tools. Veillea is Québec-first, covering SEAO and CanadaBuys, and summarizes every notice in English and French.

TenderScan at a glance (public site, checked October 12, 2026)

  • 1,500+ buying organizations monitored; 13 provinces and territories. Sources checked five times a day.
  • Named sources: CanadaBuys, Toronto Bids, SEAO (via Données Québec), plus other portals.
  • Keyword matching, regional targeting, daily digest (instant on Pro), bid calendar and tracking, bid library and templates, CSV export on Pro.

Which to choose?

  • TenderScan if you bid in several provinces today or want built-in bid management tools.
  • Veillea if your market is Québec and federal first, and you want bilingual matching and English/French summaries of French-language SEAO notices.

Veillea does not replace registering on SEAO, CanadaBuys or a buyer's portal: to get documents and bid, you still use the official platform. Summaries help you triage; the official notice always prevails.

Frequently asked questions

Does TenderScan cover SEAO?

Yes. Per its data sources page, TenderScan uses SEAO data published on Données Québec, plus CanadaBuys and other portals.

How much does TenderScan cost?

Per its site, there's a free plan; Starter ($24.49/month) and Pro ($39.49/month) are billed annually, plus taxes.

Why choose Veillea over TenderScan?

For Québec-first monitoring with English/French matching, bilingual summaries of every notice and month-to-month plans, from $0.

Does Veillea cover other provinces?

Federal (CanadaBuys) tenders are covered Canada-wide. The Ontario portal, MERX and Bids&Tenders are in development.

Can I cancel Veillea anytime?

Yes, from your account; plan changes are prorated.
