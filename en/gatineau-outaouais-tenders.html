What Veillea covers for Gatineau, honestly

Veillea covers the Outaouais through SEAO and CanadaBuys:

  • SEAO: municipalities must publish their public calls for tenders there (open procedure mandatory at $139,000 and up until December 31, 2027). Departments, agencies, school service centres, health institutions and other public bodies also publish their public tenders there.
  • CanadaBuys: federal tenders, including those delivered in the region.

What Veillea doesn't cover yet: the City of Gatineau's own website and other municipal sites (municipal-site monitoring is in development, starting with Montréal), and below-threshold contracts awarded by invitation or directly, which aren't necessarily published.

Ontario-side tenders (Ottawa) aren't covered today: the Ontario portal is in development. Federal tenders are covered through CanadaBuys.

Frequently asked questions

Does Veillea cover City of Gatineau tenders?

Yes, those published on SEAO, where municipalities must post their public calls for tenders. The city's own website isn't monitored directly; municipal sites are in development.

Which sources cover the Outaouais?

SEAO (Québec public bodies and municipalities) and CanadaBuys (federal).

What value requires a Québec municipality to publish a tender?

Until December 31, 2027, an open procedure is mandatory at $139,000 and up. Between $25,000 and that threshold, a written invitation generally applies and the notice isn't necessarily published.

Can I read French notices from the region in English?

Yes. Paid plans include an English and French summary of every notice; the official notice always prevails.

How much does Veillea cost?

Free for SEAO (weekly); Solo $29/month, Team $69/month, Enterprise $199/month, flat with no per-notice fees, plus taxes.
