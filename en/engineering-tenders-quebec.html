Where engineering and architecture mandates are published

In Québec, public bodies and municipalities publish public calls for tenders on SEAO. For 2026-2027, public tendering applies from $139,000 for services contracts of government departments, the health and education networks and municipalities. Below that, a contract can be awarded by invitation or directly and isn't necessarily published. Federal contracts are posted on CanadaBuys.

Award rules specific to these services

For architecture and engineering services related to construction work, Québec regulations exclude the lowest-compliant-price rule. Public bodies can evaluate quality and then negotiate price, use quality-price weighting, or run a design competition. The government fee tariffs for architects and engineers were repealed; they still apply only to contracts in progress on July 5, 2025 or resulting from tenders launched before that date.

  • Revenu Québec certificate (attestation): required for contracts of $25,000 or more.
  • RENA: a business listed in the register of ineligible enterprises can't bid.
  • Francization (OQLF): required for businesses with 25 or more employees in Québec.
  • AMP authorization to contract: required for services contracts and subcontracts of $1M or more (tendered or direct award).

Frequently asked questions

Where can I find engineering tenders in Québec?

On SEAO for public bodies and municipalities, on CanadaBuys for federal. Veillea monitors both.

Do engineering contracts go to the lowest bidder?

Not for architecture and engineering services related to construction work: the lowest-compliant-price rule is excluded. Quality is evaluated, with price negotiation, quality-price weighting or a design competition.

Do the government fee tariffs still apply?

They were repealed but still apply to contracts in progress on July 5, 2025 or resulting from tenders launched before that date.

Do I need AMP authorization?

Yes, for services contracts and subcontracts of $1M or more.
