Where IT tenders are published

In Québec, government departments and public bodies must publish their public calls for tenders on SEAO (Act respecting contracting by public bodies), and so must municipalities. From January 1, 2026 to December 31, 2027, the trade-agreement threshold (CFTA/QOTCA) for construction and services contracts of government departments is $139,000, and municipalities must use an open (public) procedure at $139,000 and up. For government department goods contracts (e.g., licences, equipment), the threshold is $34,700.

Federal departments and agencies post on CanadaBuys. Québec notices on SEAO are often French-only; bilingual matching catches both.

Where can I find IT tenders in Québec and Canada?

Québec public bodies and municipalities publish on SEAO; federal contracts are on CanadaBuys. Veillea monitors both and ranks notices against your profile.

From January 1, 2026 to December 31, 2027, trade-agreement thresholds for government departments are $139,000 for services and $34,700 for goods. Municipalities must use an open procedure at $139,000 and up.
