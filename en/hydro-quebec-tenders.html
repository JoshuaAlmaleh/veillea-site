How Hydro-Québec tenders work

  • Publication: Hydro-Québec appears as a buyer on SEAO. Its notices often state an indicative contract value range.
  • Documents and bidding: notices point to Hydro-Québec's own supplier portal, where you get documents and submit your bid.
  • Qualification: some contracts are limited to pre-qualified suppliers. Check the conditions on Hydro-Québec's supplier site.

Important: Veillea finds Hydro-Québec notices published on SEAO. It does not monitor Hydro-Québec's supplier portal directly, and it does not register or qualify you with Hydro-Québec.

Compared with SEAO's own alerts

SEAO's alert service ("jumelage") requires the Specialist subscription at $6.95/month per user, plus $0.35 per notice sent, and covers SEAO only. Veillea is a flat price, adds CanadaBuys and other sources, ranks every notice against your profile and summarizes it in English and French.

SEAO fees: fee schedule at seao.gouv.qc.ca, checked October 12, 2026. Veillea does not replace registering on SEAO or a buyer's own portal: to get documents and submit a bid, you still use the official platform. Summaries help you triage; the official notice always prevails.
