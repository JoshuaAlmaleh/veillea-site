How SEAO works: a plain-English guide for suppliers

Published October 12, 2026 · Updated October 12, 2026

SEAO (Système électronique d'appel d'offres) is the official website where Québec public bodies publish their calls for tenders. If you want to sell to the Québec government, a municipality, a health institution or a school board, this is where you start. Most notices are in French.

Who publishes on SEAO?

Public bodies covered by the Act respecting contracting by public bodies must publish every public call for tenders on SEAO, and so must municipalities and municipal bodies. That includes departments and agencies, health and social services institutions, education and higher-education networks, and municipalities. Crown corporations such as Hydro-Québec also publish notices there.

SEAO also publishes information on awarded contracts.

Steps for a supplier

  1. Browse notices: searching notices is part of SEAO's free basic services.
  2. Create an account for your organization to order documents and bid.
  3. Order the tender documents. Addenda issued after your order are sent at no charge.
  4. Submit your bid: online (electronic submission, "STVE") or on paper, unless the buyer requires electronic submission.
  5. Follow the results: bid opening results and awarded contracts.

What it costs

Basic services, including searching notices, are free. Other services (subscriptions, documents, matching alerts, electronic submission) are paid. See the official fee schedule on seao.gouv.qc.ca.

SEAO alerts (jumelage)

SEAO's matching runs your advanced searches daily and emails new notices. It requires a subscription. See our comparison with Veillea.

Read a SEAO notice in 5 minutes: buyer & region; contract type (goods/services/construction → sets the threshold, see thresholds guide); applicable trade agreements; closing date/time, site visit, addenda; eligibility (licences, authorizations, Revenu Québec certificate).

Checks before you bid (Hydro-Québec example [S5]): Revenu Québec certificate (at Hydro-Québec, contracts of $25,000+); AMP authorization to contract for works and services above LCOP thresholds; not listed on RENA; Charter of the French language compliance (s. 152.1). Allow lead time.

Frequently asked questions

What is SEAO?

SEAO (Système électronique d'appel d'offres) is the official site where Québec public bodies and municipalities publish their public tenders.

Is searching SEAO notices free?

Yes, searching notices is part of the free basic services. Ordering documents, subscriptions and electronic bid submission are paid; see the fee schedule on seao.gouv.qc.ca.

Who must publish tenders on SEAO?

Public bodies covered by the Act respecting contracting by public bodies, plus municipalities and municipal bodies, for their public calls for tenders.

What should I prepare before bidding?

Depending on the contract: a Revenu Québec certificate, an AMP authorization to contract, a RENA check and compliance with the Charter of the French language. Allow time to obtain them.

Can I submit a paper bid?

Generally yes, unless the buyer requires electronic submission.

Are there SEAO alerts?

Yes: SEAO's paid matching service (subscription required) or a monitoring service like Veillea, which offers a free SEAO plan and a 14-day free trial, no card.
