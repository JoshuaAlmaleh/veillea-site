Québec tender thresholds 2026-2027: what suppliers need to know

Published October 12, 2026 · Updated October 12, 2026

Thresholds set the contract value at which a public contract must go to open tender, and which trade agreements apply. They're adjusted every two years. The values below apply from January 1, 2026 to December 31, 2027.

For information only, not legal advice. Check with the Secrétariat du Conseil du trésor, the Ministry of Municipal Affairs or an advisor.

Municipalities

  • $139,000 and up: open procedure (public tender) mandatory, published on SEAO and in a newspaper.

See also: Montréal tenders.

Municipal thresholds 2026-2027: public tender threshold $139,000; construction: $139,000–<$347,400 may be limited to Québec/Ontario establishments, $347,400–<$9.2M to Canada; supply: $139,000–<$368,000 Canada, $368,000+ Canada or EU; exclusive professional services: CETA threshold $101,100; public transit services/supply: $736,000 [S3].

How to read a threshold as a supplier: entity type → contract type → above an agreement's threshold, the buyer cannot restrict the tender to local firms [S4]. Other threshold-based obligations (information publication, Revenu Québec certificate, equal-access program) were not changed in 2026 [S1].

Frequently asked questions

What is the public tender threshold for a Québec municipality in 2026-2027?

$139,000, from January 1, 2026 to December 31, 2027 (Muni-Express No. 25, Ministère des Affaires municipales).

What are the thresholds for Québec government departments?

CFTA and QOTCA: $34,700 for goods, $139,000 for services and $139,000 for construction. CETA: $368,000 for goods and services; WTO-GPA: $653,200; CETA and WTO-GPA construction: $9,200,000.

Which threshold applies to Hydro-Québec?

CFTA and QOTCA: $694,700 for goods and services and $6,943,900 for construction. The CETA goods and services threshold for public utilities such as Hydro-Québec is $736,000.

Why does construction in health and education have two thresholds?

The CFTA sets $347,400 and the QOTCA $139,000 for education, health and social services bodies and municipalities.

Are CETA thresholds fixed in dollars?

No. They are set in Special Drawing Rights (SDR) and their Canadian-dollar value is revised every two years.
